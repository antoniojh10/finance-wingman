# Disclaimer text

Put this in the review notes and the final report, not in the published
page:

> This draft was prepared with an AI assistant from the product's code and
> the information supplied by its owner. It is not legal advice and does
> not create a lawyer-client relationship. Laws differ by country and
> change; vendor facts (regions, retention, transfer mechanisms) were
> checked on the dates listed and can change. Have a qualified
> data-protection lawyer review the documents before relying on them,
> especially liability limits, consumer-law clauses, international
> transfers and any statement about regulatory compliance.

Published pages should not claim "GDPR compliant" or "certified"; they say
what the product does and let the reader judge.
